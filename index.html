Seed Authenticity Guide

Certified seed carries a sealed color tag by the State Seed Certification Agency:

Breeder Seed: Golden Yellow Tag (100% pure)
Foundation Seed: Pure White Tag (99.5% pure)
Certified Seed: Azure Blue Tag (What farmers buy)

Quick 3-Day Germination Test:

Wrap 25 seeds in a moist cloth in the shade for 3 days. If fewer than 18 sprout ($< 70\%$), return the lot to the dealer with your bill.
