Is your seed genuine?

Certified seed sold to farmers carries a tamper-evident color tag prescribed under the Indian Seeds Act.

Breeder seed — golden yellow tag (research institutes only)
Foundation seed — white tag (99.5% purity)
Registered seed — purple tag
Certified seed — azure blue tag (sold to farmers)
  • Buy only with a printed GST invoice: Ensure batch number and seed variety are clearly written on your bill.
  • Examine the Blue Tag: Look for the State Seed Certification Agency (SSCA) seal and lot number.
  • Run a quick 3-day test: Wrap 25 seeds in a wet cloth in the shade. If fewer than 18 sprout ($< 70\%$), return the lot immediately with the bill.
  • Report Fake Seeds: Call the national helpline 1800-180-1551 or notify your District Agricultural Officer (DAO).
